IntentTalk: Future-Grounded Motion Planning for Event-Aware Co-Speech Motion Generation

Supplementary_material Code

Abstract

Co-speech gesture generation has made substantial progress in motion quality and speech--motion synchronization, yet existing methods predominantly map speech directly to motion or enhance semantic information at individual temporal positions. Such formulations provide limited explicit modeling of how a semantically relevant gesture should evolve before and around its occurrence. We propose IntentTalk, a hierarchical speech-to-plan-to-motion framework that introduces a motion-grounded discrete plan as an intermediate representation between speech understanding and holistic motion generation. During training, a future motion-plan tokenizer encodes upcoming body-motion windows into discrete plan tokens, providing future-grounded supervision without requiring manually defined gesture categories or intention labels. A speech-to-plan predictor subsequently learns to infer these plans solely from speech-semantic features, enabling future-aware planning at inference without access to future motion. The predicted plans guide generation through two complementary mechanisms: an event-aware semantic gate refines when semantic body motion should be activated, while hierarchical plan-conditioned FiLM controls how the planned motion is realized across shared, body-part, and motion-token representations. Extensive experiments on BEAT2 and GES-X datasets demonstrate that IntentTalk achieves superior performance over representative state of-the-art methods, validating the effectiveness of the proposed future-grounded motion planning framework.

Method

Overview of IntentTalk. Stage I learns a motion-grounded discrete future-plan vocabulary from offset future body-motion windows and freezes the pretrained tokenizer and codebook. In Stage II, a speech-to-plan predictor infers discrete plans from speech-semantic features using the frozen codebook, while the facial stream remains directly speech-conditioned. The predicted plans guide body-motion generation through an event-aware semantic gate for when semantic motion is activated and hierarchical plan-conditioned FiLM for how the planned motion is realized. Future motion is required only for plan supervision during training, not at inference.
